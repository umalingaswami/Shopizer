# Introduction to Business Payments

Payments within the Business module of the platform encompass the processes and services responsible for managing financial transactions between customers and the e-commerce system. This includes the handling of various payment methods, processing payment requests, and securely recording transaction details to ensure the accuracy and security of funds.

# Purpose and Importance of Payments

The payment functionality is fundamental to enabling the checkout process and completing customer orders. It ensures that funds are properly authorized, captured, and recorded, which is vital for maintaining customer trust and supporting business operations. Without reliable payment processing, the order lifecycle cannot be successfully completed.

# How Payments Operate in the Platform

Payment services coordinate closely with transaction services to validate and finalize payments. This collaboration maintains the integrity of both the order and payment lifecycle. The platform supports integration with multiple payment gateways and methods, providing flexibility for customers to choose their preferred payment option.
